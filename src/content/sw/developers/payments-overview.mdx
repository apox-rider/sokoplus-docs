Malipo kwenye SokoPlus hupitia **lango la malipo** (payment gateway) (ClickPesa). Mtiririko umegawanyika katika *kuzindua* (unaunda payment intent) na *kuthibitisha* (webhook ya lango hutuambia kwamba imefanikiwa).

## Endpoints za malipo (`/api/payments`)

| Mbinu | Njia | Uthibitishaji | Maelezo |
|--------|------|------|-------------|
| POST | `/payments/initiate` | JWT | Tengeneza payment intent (agizo) |
| POST | `/payments/initiate-payout` | admin | Zindua utoaji wa malipo kwa muuzaji |
| GET | `/payments/payout-status` | vendor | Angalia hali ya utoaji wa malipo |
| GET | `/payments/vendor-balance` | vendor | Salio la mapato ya muuzaji |
| POST | `/payments/purchase-subscription` | vendor | Nunua usajili wa mwezi |
| POST | `/payments/purchase-boost` | vendor | Nunua kukuza tangazo la huduma |
| POST | `/payments/purchase-product-boost` | vendor | Nunua kukuza tangazo la bidhaa |

## Mtiririko wa malipo ya agizo

1. **Checkout** — `POST /orders/checkout` huunda agizo pamoja na payment intent.
2. **Zindua** — `POST /payments/initiate` huunda malipo ya lango kwa agizo.
3. **Lango** — mteja anakamilisha malipo (mobile money, kadi, n.k.).
4. **Webhook** — ClickPesa hupiga `/api/clickpesa/payment` na matokeo.
5. **Thibitisha** — backend husasisha shughuli na agizo, na kuunda rekodi ya escrow.

## Utoaji wa malipo (payouts)

Mapato ya muuzaji hukusanyika kama salio. Utoaji wa malipo huzinduliwa na msimamizi na kuthibitishwa kupitia webhook ya utoaji wa malipo ya ClickPesa.

<Callout type="note">
Shughuli zote hurekodi `TransactionStatus`, `PaymentStatus` na `PaymentGateway` ili kumbukumbu ya fedha iweze kufuatiliwa (auditable).
</Callout>

## Mfano wa shughuli

Sehemu muhimu:

| Sehemu | Maelezo |
|-------|-------------|
| `amount` | Kiasi kwa TZS |
| `status` | Hali ya shughuli |
| `paymentStatus` | Hali ya malipo |
| `gateway` | Lango lililotumika |
| `orderId` | Agizo linalohusika (linaweza kutokuwa na thamani) |
| `type` | Agizo / usajili wa mwezi / kukuza tangazo |

SokoPlus inaweza kuzima vipengele vyote kwa wakati halisi (runtime) kwa kutumia **swichi za vipengele** zilizohifadhiwa kwenye `PlatformSetting` chini ya funguo kama `ecommerce:<feature>`.

## Zinafanya kazi vipi

- Kila swichi ina `enabled` na `message` ya hiari.
- Middleware ya `featureEnabled(key)` inarudisha **503** pamoja na `{ message, feature, enabled: false }` kipengele kikiwa kimezimwa. `god_mode` inapita kwenye kila ulinzi; `maintenance_mode` inatumika kwa jukwaa lote lakini haitoi `/api/auth`, `/api/admin` na `/health`.
- Swichi hudhibitiwa na `superAdmin` kupitia `GET /admin/ecommerce` (orodhesha zote) na `GET/PUT /admin/ecommerce/:id` (soma/badilisha moja). `GET /admin/ecommerce/public` inaonyesha orodha ile ile bila auth ya admin (bila `message`).

## Utekelezaji upande wa seva

Swichi nyingi pia hutekelezwa kwenye safu ya routes na `featureEnabled(...)`, kwa hiyo kuzima kipengele kunarudisha **503** moja kwa moja kutoka kwa API — si tu kuficha kwenye UI:

- **`subscription`** — endpoints zote za `/api/subscriptions` (my, subscribe, cancel, upgrade, downgrade, all).
- **`boosts`** — kuorodhesha na kununua service boost (`GET /api/services/my/boosts`, `POST /api/services/boost`).
- **`coupons`** — kuunda, kusasisha, kufuta, kuorodhesha, validate na apply (`/api/coupons/*`).
- **`flash_sales`** — kujiunga kwa muuzaji (`POST /api/flash-sales/join/:flashSaleId`).
- **`referral_credits`** — endpoints za referral (`/api/referrals/my`, `/api/referrals/stats`, `/api/referrals/code`, `/api/referrals/apply`) na endpoints za mikopo (`/api/credits/my`, `/api/credits/history`, `/api/credits/redeem`).

## Payment service ikiwa imezimwa = Cash on Delivery

`payment_service` ni swichi kuu ya **malipo ya mtandaoni** pekee. Ikiwa imezimwa:

- **Malipo ya mtandaoni yamezimwa** — vipengele vya fedha vinavyohitaji payment gate (subscriptions, boosts, coupon redemption, payouts, credits) vinarudisha `503` pamoja na `"Digital payments are currently offline. Cash on delivery only."`
- **Kuweka maagizo na checkout vinaendelea** — `ordering` na `checkout` ni swichi huru. Checkout inapita kwenye payment-service gate ili maagizo yaendelee kuwekwa.
- Checkout inarudisha `requiresPayment: false` na agizo linawekwa kama **Cash on Delivery**: mshirika wa usafirishaji anakusanya wakati wa kukabidhi, na escrow bado inaundwa (kwa default) na kutolewa kwa kila suborder inapothibitishwa.

Angalia [Maagizo, rukoba ya ununuzi na kuhifadhi nafasi](./api-orders) kwa umbo la jibu la checkout.

## Hadhira

| Hadhira | Inaarifiwa wakati wa kuwasha/zima |
|----------|--------------------|
| `all` | Watumiaji wote |
| `businesses` | Wauzaji + watoa huduma |
| `none` | Hakuna (kimya) |

## Mfano

```bash
# Zima uwekaji wa maagizo kote kwenye jukwaa
curl -X PUT https://api.sokopluss.co.tz/api/admin/ecommerce/ordering \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <superadmin-token>" \
  -d '{"enabled": false, "message": "Ordering is temporarily paused."}'
```

<Callout type="warning">
Kubadilisha `maintenance_mode` au `god_mode` kunaweza kuzuia shughuli halisi za watumiaji. Kuzima `payment_service` kunasitisha malipo yote ya mtandaoni lakini kuweka maagizo ya COD kunabaki. Tumia kwa uangalifu mkubwa.
</Callout>

Taratibu mbili zinahifadhi usawa wa usafirishaji wa fedha: **escrow** (hifadhi ya malipo) na **komisheni**.

## Escrow (`/api/escrow`)

Escrow hushikilia fedha hadi usafirishaji uthibitishwe.

| Mbinu | Njia | Uthibitishaji | Maelezo |
|--------|------|------|-------------|
| GET | `/escrow/status/:orderId` | JWT | Hali ya escrow ya agizo |
| POST | `/escrow/initiate-release` | admin | Toa fedha kwa muuzaji |
| POST | `/escrow/initiate-refund` | admin | Rudisha fedha kwa mteja |
| POST | `/escrow/confirm` | admin | Thibitisha utoaji/kurudishiwa |
| POST | `/escrow/process-expired` | superAdmin | Fagia escrow zilizoisha muda |

### Mzunguko wa maisha wa escrow

1. **Imeundwa (Created)** — wakati webhook ya malipo ya agizo inapothibitishwa.
2. **Imehifadhiwa (Held)** — fedha zinakaa hadi agizo litakapowasilishwa na kuthibitishwa.
3. **Imetolewa (Released)** — fedha zinakwenda kwa muuzaji baada ya uthibitisho wa upokeaji.
4. **Imerudishiwa (Refunded)** — fedha zinarejea kwa mteja baada ya kurudishiwa fedha kuidhinishwa.

## Komisheni

Jukwaa litoza komisheni kwenye maagizo (inafuatiliwa kwenye mfano wa `Commission`).

| Sehemu | Maelezo |
|-------|-------------|
| `businessId` | Muuzaji anayepokea agizo |
| `orderId` | Agizo linalohusika |
| `amount` | Kiasi cha komisheni |
| `rate` | Kiwango cha komisheni |
| `status` | Inasubiri / imelipwa |

- Komisheni huorodheshwa chini ya `GET /admin/commissions` (superAdmin).
- Tia alama kuwa imelipwa kupitia `PUT /admin/commissions/:id/pay`.

<Callout type="note">
Ufuatiliaji wa komisheni unaweza kuwashwa au kuzimwa kupitia swichi ya `commission`.
</Callout>

## Mtiririko kamili wa fedha

```text
Buyer pays ──▶ Escrow held ──▶ Delivery confirmed ──▶ Released to vendor
                                     │
                                     └──▶ Commission recorded ──▶ Paid out
```

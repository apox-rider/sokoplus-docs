Utafutaji na ugunduzi hutumika kuendesha ukurasa wa nyumbani na ukurasa wa utafutaji.

<Callout type="note">
`/api/explore` na `/api/recommendations` zinahitaji swichi ya vipengele `featured_products`. Ikizimwa kwenye `/admin/ecommerce`, endpoints hizo hurudisha `503`.
</Callout>

## GET /search

Global search across products, services, businesses and deliverables. Supports full-text matching, filters (category, price range, region, rating), sorting and pagination.

Returns `{ results, suggestions, trending }`.

## GET /search/trending

Maswali ambayo yanaongezeka kasi sasa hivi — yanatumiwa na kisanduku cha utafutaji kwenye ukurasa wa nyumbani.

## GET /search/suggestions?q=…

Mapendekezo ya kukamilisha (autocomplete) kulingana na maandishi uliyochapa. `q` ni lazima.

## GET /explore

Kila kitu kwenye ukurasa wa "Explore": mipasho, bidhaa zinazopendwa, vibao vya faragha, bidhaa zinazopendwa hivi karibuni na categories kuu.

Kwa media uploads (selfie / hati za KYC) na usimamizi wa miradi ya showcase, ona [Pakia media & showcase](./api-showcase).

## GET /api/recommendations/…

| Path | Maelezo |
|------|---------|
| `/trending-up` | Bidhaa zilizopanda kasi (velocity) katika saa 72 zilizopita dhidi ya rekodi yake ya kawaida; threshold > 1.5 |
| `/new-arrivals` | Bidhaa zilizoundwa ndani ya siku 14, zilizopangwa kwa stock |
| `/hot-deals` | Bidhaa zilizo na punguzo ≥ 15%, zilizopangwa kwa mchanganyiko wa maagizo × ubadilishaji × punguzo |

<Callout type="note">
`/recommendations/*` zote ziko nyuma ya `featured_products` flag na zinarudisha bidhaa kutoka kwa biashara zenye hali `APPROVED` pekee.
</Callout>

## Parameters & usaidizi

| Param | Maelezo |
|-------|---------|
| `q` | Maswali ya utafutaji (inahitajika kwa `/search` na `/search/suggestions`) |
| `page` / `limit` | Ukumbaji |
| `sort` | `newest` \| `price_asc` \| `price_desc` \| `rating` \| `popularity` |
| `filters` | Hifadhi ya `category, priceRange, region, rating, isVerified` |

## ESL na ukumbaji

Majibu yote ya utafutaji na ugunduzi yanatumia ukumbaji sawia. Angalia [viwango vya vivuli vya API](./api-conventions) kwa maelezo ya `page`, `limit`, `total` na mifuniko.

Maagizo, rukoba ya ununuzi na kuhifadhi nafasi vinahusika na mzunguko wa ununuzi.

## Rukoba ya ununuzi (`/api/cart`)

| Method | Path | Auth | Maelezo |
|--------|------|------|-------------|
| POST | `/cart/add` | JWT | Ongeza/ongeza idadi ya bidhaa |
| GET | `/cart` | JWT | Pata rukoba pamoja na maelezo ya bidhaa |
| PATCH | `/cart/:id` | JWT | Sasisha idadi |
| DELETE | `/cart/:id` | JWT | Ondoa bidhaa |

<Callout type="note">
Endpoints za `/api/cart` kwa sasa zimezimwa (frozen) — router haujafungwa kwenye `app.js` (rukoba huhifadhiwa upande wa mteja). Checkout inayohitaji akaunti bado inasoma vipengee kutoka kwenye jedwali la `CartItem`. Angalia [data model](./data-model).
</Callout>

### Suborders

Checkout inagawanya agizo kuwa **suborder moja kwa kila biashara ya muuzaji** (angalia [data model](./data-model)). Kila suborder ina `status` na `recipientCode` yake. Mutations zinazofanya kazi kwenye sehemu ya muuzaji mmoja zinakubali `suborderId` ya hiari ili backend ilenge suborder sahihi badala ya agizo lote:

- `PUT /orders/:id/status` → body `{ "status": "confirmed", "suborderId": "..." }`
- `PUT /orders/:id/verify-recipient` → body `{ "recipientId": "<recipientCode>", "suborderId": "..." }`
- `POST /orders/verify-qr` → body `{ "orderId": "...", "suborderId": "...", "recipientId": "<recipientCode>" }`
- `PUT /orders/:id/deliver` → body `{ "recipientId": "<recipientCode>", "suborderId": "...", "collectedAmount": 15000 }`
- `PUT /orders/:id/negotiate` → body `{ "negotiatedAmount": 14000, "suborderId": "..." }`
- `POST /delivery/assign` → body `{ "orderId": "...", "deliveryPersonId": "...", "suborderId": "..." }`
- `PUT /delivery/:id/verify` → body `{ "recipientId": "<recipientCode>", "suborderId": "..." }`

**Payload ya QR ya usafirishaji** inasimbua suborder kama `{ v:1, o:<suborderId>, r:<recipientCode>, s:1 }` — wateja wanaoakti ni kama msimbo wa QR na endpoints za uthibitisho zinarejesha `o`/`r` kuwa `suborderId` + `recipientId`.

### Body ya malipo ya agizo

```json
{
  "hasDelivery": true,
  "deliveryAddress": "Kijitonyama, Dar es Salaam",
  "deliveryLat": -6.79,
  "deliveryLng": 39.22,
  "deliveries": [
    { "businessId": "...", "hasDelivery": true, "deliveryLocation": "Mikocheni" }
  ]
}
```

`deliveries` ni safu ya hiari ya kila biashara inayobadilisha usafirishaji kwa kila suborder. `deliveryLocation` lazima iwe kwenye zones za usafirishaji za biashara; vinginevyo `deliveryFee` ya jumla ya biashara inatumika.

### Malipo kwa wakati wa kukabidhi (Cash on Delivery)

Wakati kipengele cha `payment_service` kikiwa **kimezimwa**, malipo ya mtandaoni yamezimwa lakini kuweka maagizo na checkout zinaendelea kufanya kazi. Checkout inajibu kwa:

```json
{
  "message": "Order placed successfully",
  "order": { ... },
  "requiresPayment": false,
  "escrowCreated": true
}
```

- `requiresPayment: false` → hakuna malipo ya gateway yanayotarajiwa; agizo linawekwa kama **Cash on Delivery** na mshirika wa usafirishaji hukusanya wakati wa kukabidhi.
- `escrowCreated` → rekodi ya escrow bado inaundwa (kwa default), kwa hiyo fedha zinazokusanywa wakati wa kukabidhi huhifadhiwa na kutolewa kwa kila suborder inapothibitishwa.
- Wakati `payment_service` ikiwa **washa**, `requiresPayment: true` na malipo ya gateway (ClickPesa) yanaanzishwa badala yake.
- `POST /orders/:orderCode/pay` inarudisha `{ "requiresPayment": false }` pamoja na taarifa ya COD wakati `payment_service` ikiwa imezimwa.

### Hali za agizo

`pending` → `confirmed` → `shipped` → `delivered` | `cancelled`

- Kuthibitisha suborder kunatengeneza `recipientCode` yake (inayotumika kwa uthibitishaji wa usafirishaji wa pande tatu na payload ya QR).
- Kukabidhi suborder kunarekodi `collectedAmount` (kwa default `negotiatedAmount ?? subtotal`), inatoa sehemu ya escrow ya suborder hiyo na kumkabidhi biashara.
- Kughairi (mteja au mgeni) kinaruhusiwa tu wakati agizo liko `pending`/`confirmed`; linaweka `cancelReason` kwenye agizo na suborders na kurudisha escrow.
- `returned` inakubaliwa tu kupitia njia ya admin (`PUT /admin/orders/:orderId/status`).

<Callout type="note">
Maagizo ya wauzaji wengi yanatengeneza **msimbo mmoja wa mpokeaji kwa kila suborder**, unaothibitishwa dhidi ya sehemu ya biashara inayofanana. Safu za `OrderItem` zina nakala za lahaja (`variantId`, `variantName`, `variantType`) pamoja na `selectedSpecs`/`notes`.
</Callout>

### Usimamizi wa maagizo ya admin

| Method | Path | Auth | Maelezo |
|--------|------|------|-------------|
| GET | `/admin/orders` | admin | Orodhesha maagizo yote (yametengwa kwa eneo) |
| PUT | `/admin/orders/:orderId/status` | admin+ (`manage_orders`) | Sasisha hali |
| DELETE | `/admin/orders/:orderId` | **superAdmin** | Futa agizo (soft delete) |

**`DELETE /admin/orders/:orderId`** hufanya **soft delete**: agizo hutiwa alama ya `deletedAt`/`deletedBy` na mara moja hutoweka kwenye kila swala la maagizo (orodha ya admin, orodha ya muuzaji, orodha ya mteja, na ufuatiliaji wa umma). **Halifutwi kabisa**:

- Kufuta tena (au kutafuta id iliyofutwa) inarudisha `404`.
- Maagizo yaliyofutwa yanaonekana kwenye **Rekodi Zilizofutwa** (`GET /admin/records`) na yanaweza kurudishwa kwa `POST /admin/records/Order/:orderId/restore` au kuondolewa kabisa kwa `DELETE /admin/records/Order/:orderId/nuke`.
- Historia ya escrow, bidhaa, na fedha inahifadhiwa, kwa hivyo kufuta hakuharibu rekodi za ukaguzi au malipo.
- Jibu la mafanikio ni `200` lenye `{ "message": "Order deleted successfully", "orderId", "orderCode" }`.

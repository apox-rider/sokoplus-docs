SokoPlus lets you save and reuse payment methods so checkout is quick.

## Adding a payment method

From your dashboard, open the **Payments** tab (clients) and:

1. Tap **Add payment method**.
2. Choose the type and provider.
3. Enter the account number.
4. Save.

You can set a **default** method so it's selected automatically at checkout.

## Managing methods

- Add multiple methods.
- Edit account details.
- Remove methods you no longer use.
- Change which method is the default.

## Payment providers

SokoPlus supports payments through trusted providers in the East African market, including mobile money channels commonly used in Tanzania.

<Callout type="note">
If you pay by a method backed by escrow, your funds are held safely until you confirm your order — see [Escrow protection](/en/help/escrow-protection).
</Callout>

## A note on availability

Some payment options depend on platform settings and your region. If a payment service is not available for your order, you'll see a clear message at checkout.

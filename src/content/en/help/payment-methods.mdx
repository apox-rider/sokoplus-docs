SokoPlus lets you save and reuse payment methods so checkout is quick.

## Cash on Delivery (COD)

When online payments are switched off, **Cash on Delivery (COD)** is the payment method:

- Checkout lets you place your order without paying online.
- You pay the delivery agent **in cash** when your items arrive.
- Ordering and checkout remain fully available while payments are off.

When online payments are enabled, you'll have more options — including saved methods and mobile money — to choose from at checkout.

## Adding a payment method

From your dashboard, open the **Payments** tab (clients) and:

1. Tap **Add payment method**.
2. Choose the type and provider.
3. Enter the account number.
4. Save.

You can set a **default** method so it's selected automatically at checkout.

## Managing methods

- Add multiple methods.
- Edit account details.
- Remove methods you no longer use.
- Change which method is the default.

## Payment providers

SokoPlus supports payments through trusted providers in the East African market, including mobile money channels such as **ClickPesa** and **M-Pesa** when they are enabled.

<Callout type="note">
If you pay by a method backed by escrow, your funds are held safely until you confirm your order — see [Escrow protection](/en/help/escrow-protection).
</Callout>

## A note on availability

Some payment options depend on platform settings and your region. While online payments are off, **Cash on Delivery** is the payment method for your orders; once they're enabled, more options appear at checkout.

When a buyer places an order for your products, you manage it from the **Orders** tab.

## Order lifecycle for vendors

| Stage | What you do |
|-------|-------------|
| **Pending** | Review the order and **confirm** it. Confirmation generates the recipient ID for delivery. |
| **Confirmed** | Prepare the items for dispatch. |
| **Shipped** | Assign a **delivery partner** (or hand over to one). The order moves to shipped. |
| **Delivered** | The delivery partner verifies handover with the recipient ID or QR code. |

## Confirming an order

- Open the order and tap **Confirm**.
- The system generates a **recipient ID** used for the delivery handover.

## Assigning a delivery partner

- From the order, select a member of your **delivery team**.
- Assigning sets the order to **shipped**.
- The delivery partner sees the job in their app.

<Callout type="note">
If you don't use your own delivery team, you can arrange delivery separately and update the status when the order is out.
</Callout>

## Completing the handover

The delivery partner verifies the **recipient ID** or scans the **QR code** at the buyer's location. You can track this in real time.

## What if a buyer cancels?

- If the order is still pending, the buyer can cancel it.
- Once confirmed or shipped, handle cancellations with the buyer directly or via support.
- Stock is restored automatically when a pending order is cancelled.

## Sales reporting

Use the **sales report** (period-based) and your **Analytics** tab to track revenue, best sellers and order volume.

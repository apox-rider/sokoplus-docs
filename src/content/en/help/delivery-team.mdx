Your delivery team carries out the deliveries for your shop. Manage them from the **Delivery Team** tab.

## Adding a delivery person

1. Open **Delivery Team → Add member**.
2. Enter their full name, phone number and email.
3. The system creates a **delivery** account for them automatically, with login details you can share.

## Managing your team

- **Activate / deactivate** a member to make them available or offline.
- **Edit** their details at any time.
- **Remove** a member who leaves.

<Callout type="tip">
Share the login email and password hint securely with your delivery person so they can access their delivery app.
</Callout>

## Delivery zones

Set the areas you can deliver to and the fee for each zone:

1. Open **Delivery Zones**.
2. Add a zone with its **location name**, **delivery fee**, and **estimated time**.
3. Buyers in that zone see your delivery options at checkout.

The platform can also calculate a delivery fee for a given location, falling back to your business default if no zone matches.

## Assigning deliveries

From an order, assign a delivery partner. The partner:

- Sees the job in their app.
- Carries out the **recipient ID / QR verification** at handover.
- Marks the order delivered.

## Tracking performance

- The delivery partner's **stats** are visible to them in their app.
- You can see delivery progress on each order from your **Orders** tab.

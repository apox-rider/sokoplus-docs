Every SokoPlus order follows a clear lifecycle, and deliveries are protected by a **3-way verification** system.

When you order from more than one vendor, your order is split into **suborders** — one per vendor/shop. Each suborder has its own status and its own **recipient code** (plus a QR code at handover), so you track each part of your order separately.

## Order statuses

The statuses below apply to each suborder. An order with items from several vendors can be at different stages at the same time.

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Order placed, awaiting vendor confirmation. |
| **Confirmed** | Vendor accepted the order. |
| **Shipped** | The order is out for delivery (a delivery partner is assigned). |
| **Delivered** | You confirmed receipt (or the delivery partner verified handover). |
| **Cancelled** | The order was cancelled. |

You can see your order status in the **Orders** tab of your dashboard at any time.

## The 3-way delivery check

To make sure orders reach the right person, delivery works like this:

1. When a vendor confirms a suborder, the system generates its **recipient code**.
2. The delivery partner asks for this recipient code (or a QR code) when handing over the package.
3. The delivery partner enters/verifies the code, and you can also confirm receipt yourself from your **Orders** tab.

<Callout type="note">
If a suborder has delivery, you'll be asked for its recipient code or to scan the QR code to complete the handover.
</Callout>

## Notifications

You'll get updates as your order moves through each stage, if you have **order updates** notifications enabled.

## Something went wrong?

- If your order never arrives, contact support with your order code.
- If you received the wrong or damaged item, see [Returns & refunds](/en/help/returns-refunds).

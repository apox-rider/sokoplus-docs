SokoPlus gives vendors several tools to attract buyers and grow sales.

## Coupons

Create discount codes that buyers can apply at checkout:

- **Percentage** or **fixed amount** off
- Optional **minimum order** amount and **max discount**
- Usage limits
- Start and expiry dates

Buyers apply the code in their cart. You can see how many times each coupon was used.

## Flash sales

Run time-limited promotions with a discount percentage on selected products:

- Set a name, description, discount and start/end times.
- Buyers see a **flash sale banner** and can claim the deal before it expires.

<Callout type="note">
Flash sales and coupons are only available when the relevant platform feature is enabled.
</Callout>

## Boosts

**Product boosts** and **service boosts** give your listings extra visibility — for example, featuring them in trending sections or higher in results. Purchase a boost from your dashboard for a set period.

## Subscriptions

A **subscription** can unlock premium features for your shop (higher tiers add more capability). You can:

- Purchase a plan
- **Upgrade** or **downgrade** between tiers
- View your current plan and its status

## Referral credits

Refer new buyers and vendors to SokoPlus. When your referral signs up and becomes active, you earn **credits** you can redeem — see [Coupons, credits & referrals](/en/help/coupons-credits).

## Where to find these tools

All promotion tools live in your dashboard. Depending on the features enabled on the platform, you'll find **Coupons**, **Flash sales**, **Boosts** and **Subscription** sections there.

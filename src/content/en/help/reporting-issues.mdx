If something goes wrong — or you have an idea — SokoPlus makes it easy to tell us.

## Customer reports

A **report button** floats on every page (visible to non-admin users). Use it to submit:

| Type | Use for |
|------|---------|
| **Bug** | Something is broken or not working. |
| **Feature request** | An idea for a new feature. |
| **Complaint** | A problem with a seller or service. |
| **Other** | Anything else. |

1. Tap the report button.
2. Choose the type.
3. Write a short message (add a page or order reference if useful).
4. Submit.

Admins review reports and can update their status — the team acts on them.

## Support tickets

For questions and issues, raise a **support ticket** from the Support page:

1. Open **Support**.
2. Create a ticket with a subject and message (link an **order** if it relates to one).
3. An admin replies to your ticket.
4. Track the ticket status: open → started → in progress → resolved.

## What happens next

- Reports are reviewed and resolved or dismissed, with notes.
- Support tickets are answered directly.
- For order problems, keep your **order code** handy — it helps the team find your order quickly.

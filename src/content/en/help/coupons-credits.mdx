SokoPlus rewards participation with coupons, credits and referrals.

## Using a coupon

1. When you have a coupon code, enter it in the **coupon field at checkout**.
2. The discount is applied to your order if the coupon is valid.
3. Coupons may have conditions — minimum order amount, maximum discount, and an expiry date.

<Callout type="note">
Not all orders qualify for every coupon. Check the terms shown for the code before checkout.
</Callout>

## Where coupons come from

- Vendors create coupons to promote their shops.
- The platform occasionally runs coupon promotions.
- Coupons have a limited number of uses.

## Platform credits

Credits are a balance on your account that can be **redeemed** toward purchases.

- View your **credit balance** in your dashboard.
- See your **credit history** (earned and redeemed).
- Redeem credits when you check out.

## Referrals

Refer new users to SokoPlus:

1. Share your **referral link/code** with friends and colleagues.
2. When they sign up and become active, you earn a **referral credit**.
3. Credits accumulate and can be redeemed.

<Callout type="warning">
Referral credit is only earned for genuine new users. Spam or self-referrals may be rejected by the system.
</Callout>

## Where to find these

- **Coupons** you've created (vendors) are under your shop's coupon section.
- **Credits** and **referrals** are visible in your dashboard balance and referral sections.

Trust is the foundation of SokoPlus. Several layers of safety work together to keep buyers and sellers protected.

## Verified businesses

- Vendors and service providers are **reviewed by admins** before listing.
- Business status is enforced: unapproved businesses cannot sell.
- ID verification and selfie verification (where enabled) tie accounts to real people.

## Moderated content

- **Reviews and feedback** are approved by admins before going public.
- **Products and services** can be subject to approval.
- Categories, FAQs and platform content are admin-managed.

## Account protection

- Email and phone **OTP verification** at signup.
- Password change and account deletion are self-service.
- Suspended or disqualified accounts are blocked from the platform.
- Activity logs track key actions for accountability.

## Financial protection

- **Escrow** holds buyer funds until delivery is confirmed.
- **Refunds** are reviewed by admins.
- Commissions are tracked transparently.

## Reporting problems

Anyone can report problems — see [Reporting issues](/en/help/reporting-issues) for bugs, complaints and feature requests.

<Callout type="tip">
If you see something suspicious — a fake shop, a scam message, or abuse — report it. The team reviews every report.
</Callout>

## What SokoPlus does NOT allow

- Selling without an approved business
- Fake or misleading product listings
- Abusive messages or harassment
- Attempts to complete transactions outside the platform

Creating an account is free and takes a few minutes. Registration is a multi-step flow where you choose your role and verify your email and phone number.

## Step-by-step

1. Open the SokoPlus app and tap **Register**.
2. Choose your role:

   - **Client** — you want to buy products and book services.
   - **Vendor** — you want to sell physical products.
   - **Service provider** — you want to offer professional services.

3. Fill in your details: full name, email, password, and phone number.
4. Choose your **region and location** from the 31 Tanzanian regions.
5. Complete the **email OTP** verification — we send a 6-digit code to your inbox.
6. Complete the **phone OTP** verification — we send a 6-digit code via SMS.
7. Your account is created. Clients become active immediately; vendors and providers start as **pending** until an admin approves their business.

## What you need

- A valid email address you can access
- A phone number you can receive SMS on
- Your location details (region, district, ward)

<Callout type="tip">
Vendors and service providers: have your business details ready — business name, registration number (if you have one), and your address. You'll add these when setting up your business profile.
</Callout>

## After registration

- **Clients** can start browsing the marketplace immediately.
- **Vendors and providers** see a *Pending Approval* screen until their business application is reviewed.
- You can update your profile, avatar, and notification preferences anytime from your dashboard.

## Can't find the verification email?

Check your spam folder. If you requested a new code, wait for the resend timer before requesting again. Still stuck? Contact support from the **Support** page.

Subscriptions and boosts are the ways sellers pay for extra visibility and premium features.

## Subscriptions

A subscription plan gives your shop **more listing capacity** and a tier of features. Plans range from a free tier up to higher tiers for growing businesses.

| Tier | Typical features |
|------|------------------|
| **Free / basic** | Core selling tools with limited listings |
| **Premium / Pro** | More listing capacity, enhanced promotion and visibility |
| **Enterprise** | Advanced features and capacity for large operations |

### Managing your subscription

- **Purchase** a plan in-app from your dashboard.
- **Upgrade** or **downgrade** whenever you like.
- View your current **plan and status** at any time.

<Callout type="tip">
Pick the tier that matches your sales volume. You can upgrade during busy seasons and downgrade later.
</Callout>

## Boosts

Boosts give specific listings extra visibility for a set period:

- **Product boost** — feature a product in more prominent places.
- **Service boost** — feature a service the same way.

Buy a boost in-app from your dashboard, and the boosted listing appears more often across the marketplace.

## Purchasing

Subscriptions and boosts are purchased in-app using your payment methods. The platform confirms the purchase and activates the feature.

Service providers offer professional services — repairs, design, technical support, photography, logistics and more — to clients across Tanzania.

## 1. Register as a service provider

Register with the **Service provider** role. Like vendors, your account starts as **pending** until your business is approved.

## 2. Complete your business profile

From your dashboard, open the **Business** tab:

- Business name and description
- Contact details (email, WhatsApp, phone)
- Address and location
- Business registration / tax numbers (optional)
- Logo and cover image

## 3. Get approved

An admin reviews your application. You can check your **verification status** live from the Business tab.

<Callout type="warning">
You cannot list services until your business status is **Approved**.
</Callout>

## 4. List your services

Once approved:

- Add **services** with a title, description, base price, banner image and category.
- Set your **working hours** so clients know when you're available.
- Manage **bookings** as they come in.

## Service provider checklist

- [ ] Account approved
- [ ] Business profile complete
- [ ] At least one service listed
- [ ] Working hours set

Ready to list services? See [Managing services](/en/help/managing-services).

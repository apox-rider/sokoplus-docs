Services are how clients find and book you. Manage them from the **Services** tab.

## Adding a service

1. Open **Services → Add service**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title** and **description**
   - **Base price**
   - **Banner image**
   - **Category**
3. Save the service.

## Service categories

Services are organised into categories such as:

- Repair & Maintenance
- Graphic Design
- Technical Support
- Web Development
- Photography
- Logistics & Delivery
- Consulting
- Cleaning & Sanitization

## Managing listings

- **Edit** service details or images anytime.
- **Archive / delete** services you no longer offer.
- Admins may need to **approve** a new service before it goes live — check the status on each listing.

<Callout type="tip">
Write a clear description that explains what you deliver, how long it takes and what's included in your price. Clear services book better.
</Callout>

## Can providers also sell products?

Yes. Service providers can also list physical products through the same product tools vendors use. This is useful if your service includes physical deliverables.

## Bookings

When a client books your service, it appears in **Bookings** — see [Managing bookings](/en/help/managing-bookings).

SokoPlus has a refund process for orders that go wrong, plus escrow protection that holds your money until you confirm delivery.

## Cancelling an order

- You can cancel an order while its status is **pending**.
- If it has already been confirmed or shipped, contact the vendor or support instead.
- When an order is cancelled, product stock is restored.

## Requesting a refund

If your order is incorrect, damaged, or never arrived:

1. Open the order in your **Orders** tab.
2. Request a refund and add a reason.
3. An admin reviews the request and either **approves** or **rejects** it.

Refund statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Waiting for admin review. |
| **Approved** | Refund approved and being processed. |
| **Rejected** | Refund declined (usually with a reason). |

## How escrow protects you

For supported payment flows, money moves through an **escrow** account:

1. You pay at checkout — funds are held.
2. The vendor delivers and you confirm receipt.
3. Funds are released to the vendor.

If the order is not delivered correctly, the money can be returned to you instead.

<Callout type="warning">
Always confirm an order as delivered only when you actually have the items. Confirming early releases payment to the vendor.
</Callout>

## Not resolved?

Submit a **customer report** using the report button on the site, or raise a **support ticket** from the Support page. Include your order code so the team can find it quickly.

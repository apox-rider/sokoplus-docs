SokoPlus is a B2B/B2C industrial commerce marketplace purpose-built for Tanzania and the wider East African market. It connects industrial buyers with **verified vendors**, **service providers** and **delivery partners** across all 31 regions of the country.

## What the platform offers

| Area | What you can do |
|------|----------------|
| Marketplace | Buy and sell physical products from verified vendors |
| Services | Discover and book professional services (repairs, design, web development, logistics and more) |
| Delivery | Order with delivery support and verify handover with a QR-based 3-way check |
| Campus commerce | University vendors and student buyers on dedicated regional setups |
| Bilingual | The full platform is available in English and Swahili |

## Core ideas

1. **Verified sellers** — Vendors and service providers go through an approval process before they can list products or services.
2. **Trust through escrow** — Money moves through a protected flow, and releases to sellers only when you confirm the order was delivered correctly.
3. **Delivery verification** — Every delivery is verified with a recipient ID or QR code, so orders reach the right person.
4. **Local first** — Built around Tanzanian regions, delivery zones, and the TZS currency.

<Callout type="note">
SokoPlus is a PWA — you can install it on your phone and use it like a native app, with offline capabilities.
</Callout>

## Who uses SokoPlus?

- **Buyers (clients)** browse and order products and book services.
- **Vendors** sell physical products and manage their own delivery team.
- **Service providers** offer services and manage bookings.
- **Delivery partners** carry out deliveries and verify handover.
- **Admins** (partner, major, and super admin) run the platform day to day.

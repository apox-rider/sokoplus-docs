Your products are the heart of your shop. Manage them from the **Products** tab in your dashboard.

## Adding a product

1. Open **Products → Add product**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Name** and **description**
   - **Price** (and an optional **discount price** with an end date for offers)
   - **Stock** quantity and **unit**
   - **Category**
   - **Images** (one or more, uploaded to the platform)
3. Save the product.

## Product statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Published** | Live and visible to buyers. |
| **Pending** | Waiting for admin approval (where required). |
| **Draft** | Not yet published. |
| **Archived** | Hidden from buyers. |

## Managing stock

- Update stock quantities as items sell.
- The **Inventory** tab shows stock levels and flags **low-stock** items so you can reorder in time.
- Inventory value is shown on your dashboard overview.

## Editing & deleting

- Edit any product's details or images anytime.
- Deleting a product removes it from the marketplace (soft delete — admins can restore it).

<Callout type="tip">
Use clear photos and accurate descriptions. Products with good images and honest pricing sell better and get better reviews.
</Callout>

## Analytics

The **Analytics** tab shows sales over time and per-category performance, so you can see what's working.

Want more visibility? See [Promoting your shop](/en/help/promoting-your-shop).

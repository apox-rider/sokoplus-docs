Escrow is a money-holding mechanism that protects both buyers and sellers.

## How escrow works

1. **You pay at checkout** — your payment is recorded and held.
2. **The vendor delivers** — the order is shipped and handed over.
3. **You confirm receipt** (or the delivery partner verifies the handover).
4. **Funds are released to the vendor.**

## Why it matters for buyers

- Your money is **not sent directly to the vendor** the moment you pay.
- If the order isn't delivered correctly, the money can be **returned to you** instead of released.

## Why it matters for sellers

- Once you deliver and the buyer confirms, payment is **released automatically**.
- It removes the awkward "pay me after" conversations.

## Releasing funds

Releases can be initiated automatically when the order is confirmed, or triggered by the admin/system where required. Refunds follow the refund approval process — see [Returns & refunds](/en/help/returns-refunds).

<Callout type="danger">
Confirming an order as delivered releases funds to the vendor. Only confirm when you physically have your items.
</Callout>

## Payouts to vendors

Vendor earnings build up as a **balance** and can be paid out in bulk. Vendors can check their balance and payout status in their dashboard.

## Accounts & registration

**How do I create an account?**
Tap **Register**, choose your role (client, vendor or service provider), enter your details and verify your email and phone with the OTP codes. See [Creating your account](/en/help/create-account).

**I didn't receive my verification code.**
Check your spam folder and wait for the resend timer. If it still doesn't arrive, contact support.

**Can I have more than one role?**
Accounts have one primary role. If you need both vendor and service provider access, talk to support about your setup.

## Buying

**How do I place an order?**
Add items to your cart, check out with a delivery address and payment method, then confirm. See [Placing an order](/en/help/placing-an-order).

**How is my money protected?**
Payments use escrow where supported — funds are held until you confirm your order was delivered. See [Escrow protection](/en/help/escrow-protection).

**Can I cancel an order?**
You can cancel an order while it is **pending**. Once confirmed or shipped, contact support.

## Selling

**How do I become a vendor?**
Register with the vendor role, complete your business profile and get approved. See [Becoming a vendor](/en/help/becoming-a-vendor).

**Why is my business still pending?**
Admins review every business application. You can check your status live in your Business tab.

**How do I get paid?**
Your earnings build up as a balance and can be paid out. Track your balance and payout status in your dashboard.

## Delivery

**How does delivery verification work?**
Deliveries use a recipient ID or QR code — the delivery partner verifies handover before the order is completed. See [Completing deliveries](/en/help/completing-deliveries).

**I'm a delivery partner — how do I get jobs?**
Vendors create delivery accounts and assign you deliveries. Keep your availability **active** in your profile.

## Payments & promotions

**What payment methods are supported?**
**Cash on Delivery (COD)** is used whenever online payments are off — you pay the delivery agent in cash when your items arrive. You can also save payment methods (including mobile money like ClickPesa and M-Pesa) in your dashboard when online payments are enabled. Options depend on platform settings and your region.

**Why can I only pay Cash on Delivery?**
Online payments are temporarily switched off. While they are, **Cash on Delivery (COD)** is the payment method: you place your order as usual and pay the delivery agent in cash on arrival. Ordering and checkout remain fully available. When online payments return, you'll have more options (saved methods, mobile money) at checkout.

**How do I use a coupon?**
Enter the code in the coupon field at checkout. See [Coupons, credits & referrals](/en/help/coupons-credits).

## Getting help

**How do I contact support?**
Raise a **support ticket** from the Support page, or submit a **customer report** from the report button on any page.

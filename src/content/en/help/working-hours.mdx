Working hours tell clients (and the platform) when your business is open.

## Setting your schedule

1. Open **Working Hours** in your dashboard.
2. For each day of the week (Monday–Sunday):
   - Toggle the day **open** or **closed**.
   - Set the **open time** and **close time**.
3. Save.

## How working hours are used

- Shown on your business/shop page so clients know when you're available.
- Used by the platform to check whether your business is currently open.

<Callout type="tip">
Keep your hours accurate — especially around public holidays — so clients don't book when you're closed.
</Callout>

## Delivery consideration

If you sell physical products with delivery, your working hours also help vendors and delivery partners plan pickup times.

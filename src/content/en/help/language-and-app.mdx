SokoPlus works in **English** and **Swahili**, and it can be installed on your phone like a native app.

## Switching language

1. Tap the language switcher in the top bar (EN / SW).
2. Your choice is remembered for next time.

The whole interface — including dashboards and notifications — switches language.

## Installing the app (PWA)

SokoPlus is a Progressive Web App. You can install it so it appears on your home screen and works offline.

| Device | How to install |
|--------|----------------|
| **Android (Chrome)** | Open the site → tap the menu (⋮) → **Add to Home screen** |
| **iPhone (Safari)** | Open the site → tap the **Share** button → **Add to Home Screen** |
| **Desktop (Chrome/Edge)** | Open the site → tap the install icon in the address bar |

<Callout type="tip">
Once installed, SokoPlus launches full-screen, loads faster and keeps working with a poor connection.
</Callout>

## Notifications

You can enable push-style notifications in your browser or app settings. Inside the app, choose which types you want to receive:

- **Order updates** — order status changes and deliveries
- **Promotions** — deals and flash sales
- **General** — platform news and messages

You can turn each one on or off from your dashboard **Settings** tab.

Completing a delivery correctly is the most important part of your job. SokoPlus uses a **3-way verification** system to make sure every order reaches the right person.

## The handover flow

1. A vendor assigns a delivery to you — the order moves to **shipped**.
2. You receive the package and head to the delivery address.
3. At the location, verify the **recipient ID** or scan the **QR code**.
4. Confirm the delivery — the order is marked **delivered**.

## Verifying a recipient

- The order has a **recipient ID** generated when the vendor confirmed it.
- Ask the recipient for their recipient ID and enter it, **or**
- Scan the **QR code** on the order using the built-in scanner.
- Only confirm the delivery once you've verified the handover.

<Callout type="danger">
Never mark an order as delivered unless you have physically handed it to the recipient and verified their ID. Doing otherwise releases payment and can lead to disputes.
</Callout>

## Recipient confirmations

The buyer can also confirm receipt themselves from their Orders tab. Either way, the delivery only completes after verification.

## If you can't find the recipient

- Try contacting the buyer through the app.
- If you can't complete the delivery, notify the vendor so they can help.
- Don't leave the package unattended without agreement.

## Your stats

Your dashboard shows your delivery history and stats — completed deliveries, and your active/offline status. Keeping your stats strong helps vendors trust you with more jobs.

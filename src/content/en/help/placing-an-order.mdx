Placing an order on SokoPlus is straightforward. Here's the full flow.

## 1. Add to cart

- Open a product and tap **Add to cart**.
- Open your **cart** to review quantities. Use the quantity controls to adjust amounts.
- Remove items you no longer want.

## 2. Checkout

From the cart, tap **Checkout**. You'll confirm:

- **Delivery address** — where the order should be sent.
- **Delivery zone** — the vendor's delivery zones and fees for your location.
- **Payment method** — when online payments are off, **Cash on Delivery (COD)** is the available option: you place the order and pay the delivery agent in cash when your items arrive. If online payments are on, choose a saved method or add a new one.

<Callout type="warning">
Checkout may be temporarily unavailable if the platform has disabled ordering. You'll see a clear message if so.
</Callout>

## 3. Confirm & pay

- Review the order summary (items, delivery cost, total).
- Confirm the order. With online payments off, no payment is taken at checkout — you pay **cash on delivery** when the items arrive. If online payments are on, your payment is handled securely — see [Payment methods](/en/help/payment-methods) and [Escrow protection](/en/help/escrow-protection).
- You'll receive an order confirmation with an **order code** for tracking.

## 4. Track & receive

- Follow the order status in your **Orders** tab: pending → confirmed → shipped → delivered.
- Orders from different vendors are split into **suborders**, one per vendor. Each suborder has its own status and **recipient code** — provide the code or scan the QR code at handover.
- Confirm receipt once you have your items.

## What if the vendor doesn't deliver?

- You can **cancel** an order while it's still pending.
- If something goes wrong after delivery, request a refund or file a report — see [Returns & refunds](/en/help/returns-refunds).

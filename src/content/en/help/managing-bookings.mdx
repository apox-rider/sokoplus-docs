When a client wants your service, they send a **booking request**. You manage it from the **Bookings** tab.

## The booking flow

1. Client finds your service and taps **Book**.
2. They add a message (their name, service, and details of what they need).
3. You receive the booking in your dashboard.
4. You **accept** or **reject** the request.

## Accepting a booking

- Open the booking and review the client's message.
- Tap **Accept** to confirm you'll do the job.
- Coordinate the details (time, location) with the client directly via messaging if needed.

## Rejecting a booking

If you can't take the job, tap **Reject**. This frees the client to book another provider.

## Booking statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Waiting for your response. |
| **Accepted** | You'll do the job. |
| **Rejected** | You declined the job. |
| **Completed** | The job was finished. |

<Callout type="tip">
Respond to booking requests quickly — clients can see available providers and book elsewhere while you wait.
</Callout>

## Working hours

Your **working hours** help clients understand when you're available. Keep them up to date — see [Setting working hours](/en/help/working-hours).

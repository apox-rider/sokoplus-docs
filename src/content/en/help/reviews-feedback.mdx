Reviews and feedback help other buyers make good decisions — and they keep sellers accountable.

## Reviews

You can review:

- **Products** — after interacting with a product page.
- **Services** — after booking a service.
- **Businesses / shops** — after dealing with a shop.

A review includes a **rating** (stars) and a comment. Your reviews appear in your dashboard under **Reviews**.

## Order feedback

After an order is delivered, you can submit **feedback** about that order for the business. This is separate from a general review — it's tied to a specific order.

## Moderation

All reviews and feedback are **moderated by admins** before going public. This means:

- Spam and fake reviews are filtered out.
- Your review appears after an admin approves it.
- Sellers can't post their own promotional reviews.

| Moderation status | Meaning |
|-------------------|---------|
| **Pending** | Waiting for admin approval. |
| **Approved** | Visible on the platform. |
| **Rejected** | Removed by an admin. |

<Callout type="note">
Please be honest and constructive. Reviews that are abusive, off-topic or promotional may be rejected.
</Callout>

## Feedback as a seller

Vendors and providers can view their reviews and ratings in their dashboard, including average rating and per-product breakdowns.

Delivery partners are the people who carry out deliveries for vendors on SokoPlus.

## How you become a delivery partner

Delivery partners are **created by vendors**. When a vendor adds a member to their delivery team, the system automatically creates a **delivery** account for them with login credentials.

1. Your vendor creates your account with your name, phone and email.
2. You receive login details to share (the vendor provides them).
3. Log in to the SokoPlus app and start accepting deliveries.

<Callout type="note">
If you're an individual looking to deliver, contact vendors you'd like to work with — they can add you to their delivery team.
</Callout>

## Your delivery dashboard

As a delivery partner you have a focused dashboard:

- **Overview** — stats and pending deliveries
- **Packages** — all assigned deliveries with the verification flow
- **Profile** — your info, availability toggle, and password change

## Availability

Toggle your status **active** or **offline** from your profile. When active, vendors can assign deliveries to you.

## Notifications

You'll be notified when a vendor assigns you a delivery. Keep notifications enabled so you don't miss jobs.

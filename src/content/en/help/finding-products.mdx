SokoPlus makes it easy to find what you need, whether it's a physical product or a professional service.

## Browse by category

- **Marketplace** — browse all products with a category filter.
- **Services** — browse professional services with their own category filter.
- **Explore** — trending products, hot deals, new arrivals and top-rated items in one place.
- **Shops** — browse individual verified shops by name, category or location.

## Search

Use the search bar to find products, services and shops by name. The global search returns suggestions as you type, plus trending search terms.

## Filtering results

On the search results page you can narrow results by:

- Category
- Price
- Shop
- Sort order (newest, price, rating)

## Product details

Each product page shows:

- Image gallery
- Price (with discount price if on offer)
- Stock availability
- Add to cart / buy now
- Related products

## Service details

Each service page shows:

- Banner image
- Description
- Base price
- Provider information
- Book button

<Callout type="tip">
If a shop or product is in a category you use often, save it to your **Wishlist** so it's easy to find again.
</Callout>

## Not finding what you need?

Try the search suggestions, clear your filters, or browse a broader category. You can also submit a **feature request** using the report button on the site.

SokoPlus is built on trust. Verification confirms that accounts belong to real people and real businesses.

## Email verification

- A 6-digit code is sent to your email when you register.
- Enter the code to activate your account.
- If your email changes later, you'll be asked to verify the new address.

## Phone verification

- A 6-digit code is sent via SMS to your phone.
- Entering the code links your phone number to your account.
- You can also **link a phone number** to an existing account later from your profile settings.

## Business (ID) verification

For vendors and service providers, an admin reviews your business application. The review covers your business details and, in some cases, ID documentation.

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Your application is waiting for review. |
| **Approved** | Your business is live and can list products or services. |
| **Rejected** | Your application was declined. You'll usually receive a reason. |
| **Suspended** | Your business was suspended by an admin. |

<Callout type="warning">
You cannot list products or services until your business status is **Approved**.
</Callout>

## Why verification matters

- Buyers can trust they are dealing with real businesses.
- The platform can hold sellers accountable through feedback and reports.
- It reduces fraud, fake listings and lost deliveries.

Forgot your password? Use the **Forgot password** link on the login screen — you'll receive an OTP by email to reset it.

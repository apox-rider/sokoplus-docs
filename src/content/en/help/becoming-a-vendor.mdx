Becoming a vendor on SokoPlus lets you sell physical products to buyers across Tanzania.

## 1. Register as a vendor

Register with the **Vendor** role. Your account starts as **pending** until your business is approved.

## 2. Complete your business profile

From your dashboard, open the **Business** tab and fill in:

- Business name
- Business email, WhatsApp and office phone
- Address (city, district, ward, landmark)
- Website URL (optional)
- Business registration number (optional)
- Tax number (optional)
- Year established
- Logo and cover image
- Default delivery fee

## 3. Get approved

An admin reviews your application. You can check your verification status live from the **Business** tab.

<Callout type="warning">
You cannot list products until your business status is **Approved**.
</Callout>

## 4. Set up shop

Once approved:

- Add your **products** (name, price, stock, unit, images).
- Set up your **delivery zones** and fees.
- Add **delivery team** members if you plan to fulfil orders yourself.
- Set your **working hours**.

## Vendor checklist

- [ ] Account approved
- [ ] Business profile complete
- [ ] At least one product listed
- [ ] Delivery zones configured
- [ ] Working hours set

Ready to list products? See [Managing products](/en/help/managing-products).

Search and discovery power the marketplace home page, search overlay and explore page.

## Global search (`/api/search`)

| Method | Path | Auth | Description |
|--------|------|------|-------------|
| GET | `/search` | JWT + `use_global_search` permission | Full-text search across products, services and businesses |
| GET | `/search/trending` | — | Trending search terms |
| GET | `/search/suggestions` | — | Autocomplete suggestions for a partial query |

`GET /search` requires the caller to hold the `use_global_search` permission (assigned to admins by default; assignable via `AdminPermission`).

## Explore feed (`/api/explore`)

| Method | Path | Auth | Description |
|--------|------|------|-------------|
| GET | `/explore` | — | Aggregated discovery feed (categories, hot deals, new arrivals, top-rated, nearby) |

`GET /explore` is gated by the `featured_products` feature flag.

## Curated product feeds (`/api/recommendations`)

| Method | Path | Auth | Description |
|--------|------|------|-------------|
| GET | `/recommendations/new-arrivals` | — | Products created within 14 days, ranked by stock |
| GET | `/recommendations/hot-deals` | — | Products with ≥15% discount, scored by orders × conversion × discount |
| GET | `/recommendations/trending-up` | — | Products whose 72-hour order/cart velocity exceeds their lifetime baseline (threshold > 1.5) |

All three feeds are gated by the `featured_products` feature flag and only surface products from `APPROVED` businesses.

<Callout type="tip">
The same curated classifications are also exposed under `/api/products` — see [Products](./api-products).
</Callout>

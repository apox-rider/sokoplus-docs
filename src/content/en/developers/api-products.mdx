Products are the physical goods sold by vendors.

## GET /products/all

Public product listing.

| Query | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `category` | Filter by category slug |
| `search` | Search by name/description |
| `limit` | Max results (default 20) |

## GET /products/:id

Single product detail. Increments the product `viewCount`.

{

## Vendor product endpoints

| Method | Path | Auth | Description |
|--------|------|------|-------------|
| POST | `/products/add` | vendor+ | Create product (multipart images) |
| PUT | `/products/:id` | vendor+ | Update product |
| DELETE | `/products/:id` | vendor+ | Soft-delete product |
| GET | `/products/myProducts` | vendor+ | Your products |
| POST | `/products/qr/:id` | vendor+ | Generate product QR code |
| POST | `/products/image` | vendor+ | Upload product image |

<Callout type="warning">
Business approval is required — vendors without an `APPROVED` business get a `403`.
</Callout>

## Boosts

- `POST /products/boost` — purchase a product boost.
- `GET /products/view-boosts` — active boosts.
- `GET /products/category-analytics` — category performance (revenue = stock × price).

## Trending endpoints

| Path | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `GET /products/trending` | Trending products |
| `GET /products/hot-deals` | Discounted products (≥ 15% off) |
| `GET /products/new-arrivals` | Products from the last 14 days |
| `GET /products/top-rated` | Top-rated products |

<Callout type="note">
These trending feeds are also aggregated in `GET /explore` and `GET /recommendations/*`.
</Callout>
